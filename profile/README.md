## Who we are

AraguaneyCode is a software studio from Maracay, Venezuela. We build the technology that small and growing businesses need to answer customers, take payments and keep their operations in order.

The araguaney is Venezuela's national tree. It blooms in the middle of the dry season, when everything else is bare. That is how we see our work: software that helps businesses grow in real conditions, not ideal ones.

## What we build

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Custom systems** | Inventory, billing, collections and multi-currency payments, built around how your business actually works. |
| **WhatsApp assistants** | Assistants that answer questions, book appointments, send reminders and confirm payments, connected to your calendar and your bank. |
| **Websites and landing pages** | Fast, bilingual sites that turn visits into conversations. |
| **Mobile apps** | Your system, in your customers' and your team's pocket. |

## How we work

1. **Talk** · A free 30-minute diagnosis of your problem.
2. **Propose** · A written proposal with scope, timeline and price within 48 hours.
3. **Design** · A prototype you approve before we write code.
4. **Build** · Weekly progress you can see.
5. **Support** · Delivery, training and a maintenance plan.
